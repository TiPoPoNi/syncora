### Problem Statement

In many organizations, project execution involves the coordination of multiple **clients, workers, vendors, resources, tasks, and deadlines**. Managing these activities manually or through disconnected tools can make it difficult for project managers to maintain an accurate view of the overall project schedule.

A project often consists of multiple tasks that have dependencies on one another. For example, a worker may not be able to begin a task until a vendor has delivered the required materials. Similarly, a delay in one task can affect several subsequent tasks and may eventually cause the project to miss its final client deadline.

The major problems faced in such situations include:

- Difficulty in managing multiple projects and their deadlines.
- Inefficient allocation of workers and other resources.
- Conflicts caused by assigning the same resource to multiple tasks simultaneously.
- Lack of visibility into task dependencies and their impact on the overall schedule.
- Difficulty in identifying which tasks are critical to project completion.
- Delayed detection of vendor, worker, or task-related delays.
- Difficulty in understanding how one delay propagates to dependent tasks.
- Manual rescheduling when resources become unavailable or deadlines change.
- Lack of centralized information about clients, vendors, workers, projects, and resources.
- Limited decision support for managers when choosing alternative resources or schedules.

Traditional project management systems often focus primarily on **recording tasks and tracking progress**, rather than understanding the relationships between tasks, resources, dependencies, and deadlines.

Therefore, there is a need for a centralized **Project Resource Allocation & Scheduling System** that can integrate project information, manage resource allocation, analyze task dependencies, detect scheduling conflicts, identify critical activities, track deadline risks, and assist project managers in creating and maintaining efficient project schedules.

The proposed system aims to address these challenges by providing a **dependency-aware and resource-aware scheduling platform** that enables organizations to better coordinate their projects and improve the probability of completing them within their planned deadlines.
