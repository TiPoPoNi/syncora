## Proposed Solution

The proposed **Project Resource Allocation & Scheduling System** will provide a centralized platform for managing and coordinating all major activities involved in project execution.

The system will allow organizations to manage **clients, projects, tasks, workers, vendors, resources, deadlines, and task dependencies** through a single platform. Project managers will be able to create projects, divide them into tasks and milestones, assign appropriate workers and vendors, define dependencies between tasks, and establish project deadlines.

A scheduling engine will analyze **task dependencies, resource availability, task duration, priorities, and deadlines** to create an organized project schedule. The system will continuously monitor the schedule and identify issues such as resource conflicts, overdue tasks, unavailable workers, vendor delays, and projects that are at risk of missing their deadlines.

The system will also analyze the impact of delays on dependent tasks. When a task or resource is delayed, the system can determine which subsequent activities may be affected and provide an updated view of the expected project completion date.

The project will use scheduling and optimization concepts such as **dependency analysis, resource allocation, priority-based scheduling, and Critical Path Method (CPM)** to support better project planning and decision-making.

In later development phases, an intelligent layer can be introduced to provide **resource recommendations, project-risk analysis, delay prediction, schedule optimization, and natural-language assistance**. These recommendations will remain under manager control, following a **human-in-the-loop approach**.

The overall solution aims to provide organizations with a structured, centralized, and intelligent platform that improves **resource utilization, schedule visibility, deadline management, and project execution efficiency**.
