## Objectives

The primary objective of the **Project Resource Allocation & Scheduling System** is to develop a centralized platform that helps organizations efficiently plan, schedule, allocate, and monitor project resources while maintaining control over tasks and deadlines.

The specific objectives are:

1. **Centralize project information** by managing clients, projects, tasks, workers, vendors, resources, and deadlines within a single platform.
    
2. **Improve resource allocation** by assigning suitable workers, vendors, and other resources according to their availability, skills, workload, and project requirements.
    
3. **Manage task dependencies** by representing relationships between tasks and ensuring that dependent activities are scheduled appropriately.
    
4. **Automate project scheduling** by considering task duration, dependencies, resource availability, priorities, and project deadlines.
    
5. **Detect resource conflicts** such as assigning the same worker or resource to multiple overlapping tasks.
    
6. **Monitor project deadlines** and identify overdue or potentially delayed activities.
    
7. **Analyze delay propagation** to determine how a delay in one task, vendor, or resource can affect dependent tasks and the overall project completion date.
    
8. **Identify critical activities** using scheduling techniques such as the Critical Path Method (CPM).
    
9. **Improve project visibility** through dashboards, calendars, Gantt charts, progress indicators, and reports.
    
10. **Support managerial decision-making** by providing information about resource utilization, project risks, scheduling conflicts, and alternative resource allocations.
    
11. **Provide intelligent recommendations in future versions** for resource assignment, schedule optimization, delay prediction, and project-risk analysis.
    
12. **Maintain security and accountability** through authentication, role-based access control, and activity/audit tracking.
    
13. **Improve overall project execution efficiency** by reducing scheduling conflicts, improving resource utilization, and helping organizations complete projects within planned deadlines.
