### Project Overview

The **Project Resource Allocation & Scheduling System** is a full-stack business management platform designed to help organizations efficiently manage and coordinate **clients, projects, tasks, workers, vendors, resources, dependencies, and deadlines** from a centralized system.

In real-world projects, multiple people and organizations are often involved in completing a single project. A client may provide requirements and deadlines, vendors may supply materials or services, and workers may perform different tasks. These activities are interconnected, meaning that a delay or unavailability of one resource can affect several other tasks and ultimately impact the final project deadline.

The proposed system addresses this problem by providing a centralized platform where project managers can **create and monitor projects, divide projects into tasks, allocate resources, assign workers and vendors, define task dependencies, establish deadlines, and track progress**.

The system will include a scheduling engine capable of analyzing task dependencies, resource availability, deadlines, and priorities to generate and maintain an effective project schedule. It will detect **resource conflicts, overdue tasks, scheduling problems, and potential deadline risks**.

A key feature of the system will be its ability to understand the impact of delays. For example, if a vendor delivers required material three days late, the system can identify the tasks dependent on that material, determine how the delay propagates through the project, and indicate whether the final client deadline is at risk.

The platform will also use **Critical Path Method (CPM)** and other scheduling algorithms to identify critical tasks and help managers understand which activities have the greatest impact on project completion.

In the advanced stage, an **intelligent decision-support layer** will be added to recommend suitable resources, identify project risks, suggest alternative schedules, and assist managers in making better resource-allocation and scheduling decisions.

The overall goal is to transform traditional project management from simple task tracking into a **dependency-aware, resource-aware, and intelligent scheduling system** that helps organizations complete projects efficiently and within their planned deadlines.
